This notice is sent to you in accordance with the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act. Example Motors Company has decided that a defect which relates to motor vehicle safety exists in certain 2023-2025 Example Rover vehicles, including yours.

What is the problem?
The rear-view camera image may not appear when the vehicle is put in reverse. A rear-view camera that does not display an image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.

What will we do?
Your dealer will update the camera software. The repair takes about 45 minutes and will be done free of charge.

What should you do?
Call your Example Motors dealer to make an appointment. Please bring this notice with you.

Reimbursement
If you paid to have this problem repaired before you received this notice, you may be eligible for reimbursement. Keep your receipt; for example, a dealer charge of $185.00 for a camera software update may qualify.

Questions?
Call Example Motors Customer Care at 1-888-555-0131.
If you believe the dealer has failed or is unable to remedy the defect without charge, you may contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration at 1-888-555-0199 or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
