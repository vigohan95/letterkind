Please verify your identity so we can process your tax return

We received a federal income tax return, Form 1040, for the tax year shown above with your name and taxpayer identification number. The return claims a refund of $2,184.00.

To protect you from identity theft, we need to verify your identity before we process the return. We will not issue the refund or apply any overpayment until you respond.

What you need to do
Verify your identity within 30 days of the date of this letter, using one of these options:

1. Online, through the Identity and Tax Return Verification Service. You will need this letter and a copy of the return, if you filed one.
2. By phone, at 1-800-555-0119, between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. local time, Monday through Friday. Have this letter, the return for the tax year shown above, and a prior-year return available.

If you did not file this return
Tell us right away, online or at the phone number above. Someone may have used your information to file a return without your permission.

What happens if you don't respond
We can't process the return, issue a refund, or credit any overpayment to your account until we hear from you.

We never ask for personal or financial information by e-mail, text message, or social media.
